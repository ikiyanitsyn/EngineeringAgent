Steel correlations. For aluminium, titanium, polymers and composites supply a tested fatigue strength with 'endurance_test' (taken as Se' at the stated life).

Compressive mean stress is treated as zero mean (conservative for Goodman).
Valid for 10^3 to N_endurance cycles; below Se the life is reported as infinite (steel) and above f*Sut the result is low-cycle fatigue (not valid here).

Plan: a printable PLA plug for a 10 mm push-to-connect fitting. A 10 mm stem (20 mm long, chamfered tip) goes into the fitting and is gripped by its collet; a 14 mm collar stops it at the right depth; an 18 mm knob (chamfered top edge) lets you pull it out by hand (press the fitting's release ring first). It prints standing on the flat knob face, no supports. Check: 30 psi (0.207 MPa) water pressure on the stem tip, the stem held where the collet grips; compared with the plain axial stress p x A / A = 0.207 MPa as an order-of-magnitude estimate.

```python
from build123d import *
from engagent.cad import params, save
P = params(stem_d=10.0, stem_len=20.0, collar_d=14.0, collar_h=3.0, knob_d=18.0, knob_h=10.0, chamfer=0.8)
a = (Align.CENTER, Align.CENTER, Align.MIN)
knob = Cylinder(P.knob_d / 2, P.knob_h, align=a)
knob = chamfer(knob.edges().group_by(Axis.Z)[-1], 1.5)   # easy to grip, prints without support
collar = Pos(0, 0, P.knob_h) * Cylinder(P.collar_d / 2, P.collar_h, align=a)
stem = Pos(0, 0, P.knob_h + P.collar_h) * Cylinder(P.stem_d / 2, P.stem_len, align=a)
stem = chamfer(stem.edges().group_by(Axis.Z)[-1], P.chamfer)
save(knob + collar + stem)
```
